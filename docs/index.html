Business Support Programme

Solex.

A nationwide grant initiative empowering Nigerian micro, small, and medium business owners through direct capital injection.

CATEGORY A
Unregistered Businesses

One-time grant support for everyday market traders, artisans, food vendors, POS agents, and informal micro-enterprises. CAC registration is not required.

CATEGORY B
Registered Businesses

One-time enterprise support for formally incorporated Nigerian enterprises. Requires valid CAC Registration Number and verified CAC Certificate.

Programme Schedule: Commencing 10 December 2026. Disbursements scheduled for December 2026 following independent administrative assessment.
